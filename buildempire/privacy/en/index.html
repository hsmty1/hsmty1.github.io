Introduction

K.H.App ("the developer") publishes the iOS app BUILD EMPIRE: Construction ("the app"). This policy explains how information is handled in the app. This page covers this app only; the developer's other apps each have their own terms.

In short: the app never receives anything that identifies you — no name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth. Text you type inside the game, such as your company name or the names of your buildings, is never sent to the developer either.

1What the developer does not collect

There is no account and no sign-in. The developer does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or calendar — the app never asks for these permissions
  • Precise location (GPS)
  • Health data
  • Text you enter in the game, such as company, project or staff names

2Save data

Your save data — company name, cash, sites, headcount and the rest of your progress — is stored on your device. There is no developer server, and the developer never receives your saves.

So that a new phone or a lost device does not cost you years of progress, a copy is also kept in Apple's iCloud (key-value storage). This is Apple's own mechanism, and the copy is shared only between your own devices signed in to the same Apple ID. The developer cannot read it. If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is synced and your save stays on the device alone.

Deleting your data: deleting a save from the "Save data" screen on the title screen removes both the file on your device and the copy in iCloud. Uninstalling the app alone may leave the iCloud copy behind, so delete your saves inside the app first if you want everything gone.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you play for free, the app shows advertising from Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner at the bottom of the screen, and video adverts you choose to watch.

To serve these adverts, Google may collect the following from your device. It is handled under Google's own privacy policy, and the developer does not receive any of it individually.

  • Device type, OS version, language and country
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • Records of adverts shown and tapped
  • An approximate region inferred from your IP address

Tracking permission: the app shows the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", no tracking using the advertising identifier takes place. Your choice makes no difference to the game itself. You can change it at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Buying the one-off Premium upgrade stops adverts being shown at all.

See also: Google Privacy Policy / AdMob and privacy

4Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

To learn which features are used and where people stop playing — which is how updates get prioritised — the app uses Firebase Analytics (Google LLC). These are the only events sent:

game_startA new company was founded (difficulty, founder trait)
tutorial_doneThe introduction was finished
project_startA job was won (tier; design or construction)
stage_upA stage was reached (month, completed jobs)
store_viewThe store screen was opened
purchasePremium was purchased (no amount, no payment details)
run_endHow a company ended (world number one, bankruptcy…)
session_endThe screen you were on when you closed the app, and your progress

Every one of these consists of numbers and a few fixed words (such as the difficulty name). Nothing you typed is included. They are treated as statistics that do not identify you.

See also: Firebase and privacy

5In-app purchase

The app offers a one-off "Premium" upgrade. All payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. Card details never reach the developer.

Whether you own the upgrade is determined on your device, using Apple's StoreKit. No purchase history is stored on any developer server. On the same Apple ID you can restore it from "Restore purchase" in the store menu.

6Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, the developer does not share or sell your information to third parties. Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics) and Apple (iCloud, App Store) handle information under their own policies in order to provide those services.

7Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been sent, please contact us at the address below and we will act on it promptly.

8Access and deletion requests

Because the developer holds nothing that identifies you, there is no personal record to hand over. Your save data can be deleted inside the app at any time, as described above. For requests about advertising or analytics data, please contact us below or the relevant company directly.

9Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as the law or the app changes. Where a change is significant, the date at the top of this page will be updated and the change will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
